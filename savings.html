Straight answers

The questions everyone asks.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Really free, and the catch is… there isn't one. When the card saves you money, the pharmacy network pays us a small fee. You never pay us a cent, at the counter or ever. We only win when you save — that's the whole model, and it's why we show it to you openly.

Is this insurance?

No. It's a discount card — it gives you access to negotiated cash prices at participating pharmacies. It doesn't pay for anything, doesn't have premiums, and doesn't replace insurance. If you have insurance, use whichever price is lower.

Can I use it with my insurance or Medicare/Medicaid?

You can compare it against private insurance and use whichever price is lower — but you can't combine them on one purchase. If you have Medicare or Medicaid, discount cards can't be used with your coverage, so this card isn't for you.

Does the AI assistant give medical advice?

No — savings information only. It will never tell you to start, stop, or change a medication. Those decisions belong to your pharmacist and doctor, who know your full picture. The AI just shows you where the money hides.

Why is an AI company doing a pharmacy card?

Because our founder was a pharmacy cashier, she watched people pay full price who couldn't afford it, and she built the kind of tool she wished she could have handed across the counter. We research the deals, the AI explains them, and you keep the savings. Balling on a Budget — that's the whole brand.
