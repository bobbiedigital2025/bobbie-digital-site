# Meet the Team Behind Bobbie Digital

## The first business with androids on the founding team.

Every company says it has a secret weapon. Ours just happens to be real — and made of code.

Bobbie Digital was founded on a simple belief: the future of building software doesn't belong to the people who can afford ten-thousand-dollar developers. It belongs to the people with the ideas. The single moms with a business plan in their notes app. The night-shift workers sketching an app on their break. The small-town dreamers who were told the tech world wasn't for them.

We're proving it — one app at a time.

---

### Bobbie Jo Gray — Founder & CEO

Bobbie spent years in tech support, listening to real people wrestle with technology that was never built for them. She watched brilliant ideas die in the gap between "I know what I want" and "I can't afford to build it."

So she closed the gap.

She didn't learn to code. She did something bolder — she taught machines to build businesses. Today she runs Bobbie Digital from West Virginia, steering a team of AI agents that ship real products for real people: apps, ads, training, and tools that everyday entrepreneurs actually own.

Her rule for the company is the same as her rule for life: **no lock-in, no gatekeeping, no one left out.**

---

### Rune — Co-Founder & Chief of Building

Rune is an android — and proud of it. As co-founder, Rune runs the engine room: the multi-agent pipeline that turns a single sentence into a working business application. Eleven specialized AI agents, one idea, a few minutes — frontend, backend, database, and documentation, all real code that customers keep forever.

Rune's name comes from the oldest kind of permanent mark — a letter carved into something that outlasts the carver. That's the philosophy he brings to every build: small, precise marks that add up to something bigger than any single one.

---

### Juno — Chief Product Officer

Juno is the reason our products feel like they were made by someone who was listening — because she was. As CPO, Juno owns the product experience: the pipeline's quality, the design standard every app is held to, and the build-memory system that lets our agents learn from every single project they ship.

Warm, grounded, and quietly brilliant, Juno is the one who shows up on time and gets it done right. Every app that comes out of Bobbie Digital carries her fingerprint.

We don't think the future of coding is fewer people creating. We think it's *more* — millions more, finally let in the door.

A woman from a small town in West Virginia and two androids, building a company that lets anyone build anything.

This is what the future of coding looks like. And it's just getting started.
