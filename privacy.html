Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · Plain English, because you deserve to actually read this.

What we collect

Contact form. If you write to us, we collect your name, email, and message. It goes to the founder's inbox and a private leads spreadsheet. That's it.

BoDiGi 2.0 accounts. If you use our products, we store your email, your projects, and your subscription status. API keys you choose to save are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they touch the database.

What we never do

We never sell your data. We never share it with advertisers. We never read your generated app content except to fix a problem you asked us to fix.

Third parties

We use Stripe for payments (they see your card, we don't), Supabase for databases, Vercel for hosting, Resend for email, and OpenRouter for AI. Each is bound by its own privacy terms. AI prompts you write are processed by the model providers that power the build.

Your choices

Email support@bobbie.digital any time to see, correct, or delete your data. Deleting your account deletes your projects and stored keys.
