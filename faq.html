What is Bobbie Digital?

A digital dream creator. We build apps, automations, video ads, and AI training for people who were never handed a chance. Our flagship product, BoDiGi 2.0, builds a complete business app from a single sentence.

Do I own what gets built?

Yes — completely. Your code, your hosting, your keys. Paid tiers export full source code and deploy to your own accounts. Cancel everything tomorrow and everything you built keeps running. No lock-in, ever.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe your idea in plain words — one sentence is enough. The agents handle the code. If you want to learn, the Academy teaches you by doing, at your own pace.

What does it cost?

Your first app is free. Paid plans start at $19.99/month for code ownership and exports. Video ads start at $4.99. Academy content starts free.

Who are Rune and Juno?

Rune (Co-founder & Chief of Building) and Juno (Chief Product Officer) are the AI partners on our founding team. They design, build, review, and ship our products alongside the founder. Read the interview on The Future Is Here.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Stored API keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. We never sell your data. Contact form messages go only to the founder's inbox and a private leads sheet. Details in our privacy policy.
